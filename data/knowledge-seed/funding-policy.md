# Expansion funding policy

The fund commits at most 180 million dollars to any single terminal programme, and
requires a demand score above 60 in the national peer set before a proposal is reviewed.

## Slot-controlled fields

A slot-controlled airport is reviewed under a separate track. Capacity added there does
not convert to movements without a slot allocation, so the demand gap overstates the
available upside and the committee applies a 40 percent haircut to it.

## Cargo-dominant fields

Where freight exceeds 60 percent of revenue tonne-kilometres, passenger terminal
expansion is not funded from this programme at all.
